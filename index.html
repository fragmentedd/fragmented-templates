Our story

Started with one oven and a Sunday habit.

Maple & Thyme began as a farmers-market stall, three sourdough loaves and a folding table. Nine years later, the ovens run before sunrise and the same neighbors still stop by — some now with kids of their own in tow.

Everything on the menu is made in-house, in small batches, using flour milled upstate and produce from growers we know by name.
